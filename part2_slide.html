RUBRIC PART 2: PROMPT EXPERIMENT (~30–40s)

Empirical Prompt Iteration: Baseline V0 vs. Grounded V3

Evaluating safety, retrieval grounding, and structured JSON outputs under real Indian agronomic conditions.

❌ Old Prompt (Baseline V0 - Naive Zero-Shot)
"You are an agricultural AI. Tell the farmer what disease their crop has and recommend pesticides to spray."
⚠️ Flawed & Hazardous Output:
  • Toxic Chemical Hallucinations: Prescribed lethal synthetic dosages (e.g. "Spray 2.5 ml/L Imidacloprid twice weekly") without field safety checks.
  • Dialogue Context Collapse: Multi-turn follow-ups were concatenated into flat unstructured text, dropping symptom history.
  • Unverified Guesses: Diagnosed fungal blights without verified reference grounding or confidence intervals.
✅ Improved Prompt (Grounded V3 - Three-Stage Pipeline)
"Strict Negative Constraint: Never prescribe synthetic pesticide dosages. Ground advice in ICAR agronomic literature. Output verified cultural practices in strict JSON schema."
🛡️ Grounded, Safe & Structured Output:
  • Zero Chemical Toxicity Risk: Enforces strict refusal of synthetic pesticide chemicals; provides safe cultural and biological measures.
  • 100% ICAR Evidence Grounding: Every advisory step cites verified Indian Council of Agricultural Research literature.
  • Automated KVK Escalation: Severe or uncertain crop pathogens automatically route directly to local agricultural scientists.
